# Design System: MailCraft Gallery

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Fifteen Brands, Fifteen Hands"**

The gallery is a collection, not an identity. Each of the fifteen templates is the email one invented brand would really send, and each is built from an artefact of that brand's own world: a ledger pad, an eye chart, a sampler, a transit diagram, a VU meter bridge, a seed packet, a lowbrow gallery panel, a forge floor, a trail blaze, a rail ticket, a thermal receipt, a shipping label, a conference programme, a provenance ribbon, a CRT high-score screen. No palette, typeface or layout formula is shared across brands; what is shared is the set of construction rules below, which every template obeys and which let any of them survive the worst inbox.

The system therefore lives at two levels. The **house rules** (Layout, Shapes, Components, Do's and Don'ts) are invariant: body-only block JSON, table rendering, square corners, a 40 px content inset, display type in heading blocks only, owned rasters, and a system-font fallback that still reads in-world. The **brand register** (Colors, Typography, and the per-brand tokens in the frontmatter) records each own-world: its artefact colours at page scale, its type pairing with fallback stack, its signature device and its assets.

Density is the artefact's own: a receipt is tight and monospaced, an eye chart is airy and centred, a programme is a grid. The action is found inside the artefact (the ledger's total line, the eye chart's last line, the ticket's stub, the receipt's footer), never in a generic slab bolted on below. The category default the old gallery shipped (photo hero with centred headline and pill button, a row of icon cards, a dark CTA slab, an italic quote) is the confirmed anti-reference.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One brand per template, one artefact per brand, committed at page scale.
- Body-only block JSON rendered as tables with inline CSS; 600 px wide (560 and 380 where the artefact is narrower).
- Square corners everywhere; no shadows; depth comes from outer ground versus page and from ruled bands.
- Display type lives in heading blocks with a named email fallback stack; text blocks carry only body copy.
- Photos, art and any lettering are owned, provenance-tagged rasters with alt text.
- Realistic sample content: names, prices, codes and dates, never merge tags.

## Colors

Every brand pairs an outer ground with a page and commits its artefact's real colours at full page scale; there is no shared palette.

The frontmatter keys every colour by brand (`<brand>-<role>`). In each brand the first two tokens are the outer ground (`settings.backgroundColor`) and the page (`settings.bodyBackgroundColor`), the third is the ink or the dominant artefact colour, and the rest are the artefact's accents and rules.

### Primary
- **Per-brand artefact colour**: Ledger Ink, Optotype Black, Indigo Vat, Line Yellow, Meter Ivory, Tomato Red, Candy Yellow on Panel Outline Black, Heat Orange, Blaze Red, Rail Red, Thermal Ink, Kraft, Signal Blue, Provenance Ribbon Red, Sprite Yellow. Each is used where the artefact uses it: the ledger's rules, the transit line's spine, the ticket's header band, the ribbon's recorded segments.

### Secondary
- **Artefact accents**: the duochrome red/green of the eye chart, the three dye colours of the sampler, the signpost yellow of the trail, the stub tint of the ticket, the unrecorded-gap buff of the provenance ribbon, the 16-colour sprite palette of the arcade (seven sprite colours used, one per score row).

### Neutral
- **Outer grounds**: dark or muted surrounds chosen from the brand's setting (Binding Green, Exam Room Wall, Undyed Linen, Asphalt, Studio Black, Leaf Green, Kustom Teal, Soot, Slate, Platform Steel, Counter Grey, Corrugated Board, Venue Wall, Saleroom Walnut, Bezel Black).
- **Secondary inks and rules**: each brand carries one softer ink for labels, captions and the postal footer (Faded Ledger Ink, Acuity Grey, Kilometre Grey, Dimmed Ivory, Ash, Scree Grey, Field Grey, Speaker Grey, Catalogue Grey, Sprite Lavender) and its own rule colour.

### Named Rules
**The Artefact Palette Rule.** A brand's colours are the colours of its artefact (ledger green, packet tomato and leaf, rail red, phosphor black), applied at page scale. No colour is borrowed from another brand in the set, and no brand gets a neutral SaaS palette.

**The Readable Ink Rule.** Body text and secondary ink hold at least 4.5:1 against the surface they sit on (the softest shipped pairing, Acuity Grey #767676 on white, is 4.54:1). Display lettering of 22 px bold and up may sit on a saturated artefact field at the large-text threshold.

## Typography

**Display Font:** per brand, in heading blocks only (see the frontmatter `<brand>-display` entries).
**Body Font:** per brand, the template's `defaultFont` at its `defaultFontSize` (15 to 17 px; the 380 px receipt runs at 14 px).
**Label/Mono Font:** Courier Prime or Courier New where the artefact is typed (Ledgerline folio and times, Paper Plane label lines, Margin Books throughout).

**Character:** fifteen voices, each the typeface the artefact would really carry: Georgia ledger serif, Helvetica optotypes and timetables, Silkscreen cross-stitch, Barlow Condensed meter legends, Alfa Slab One packet print, Bungee kustom lettering, Big Shoulders Stencil batch marks, Overpass trail signage, Courier receipt print, Archivo label and programme grotesque, EB Garamond catalogue serif, Press Start 2P arcade pixels.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (400 to 800, 24 to 96 px, line-height 1 to 1.6): the brand's hook and artefact lettering: the eye chart's E, the stencil batch mark, the programme title, the high-score heading.
- **Headline** (400 to 800, 20 to 26 px): section heads inside the artefact (Provenance, This month along the line, How to save it).
- **Title** (600 to 800, 15 to 22 px): row titles in data rows: entries, sessions, stops, lots, crops.
- **Body** (400, 15 to 18 px, line-height 1.35 to 1.7 where set): running copy in text blocks; colour and size set per block, nothing else.
- **Label** (400 to 700, 11 to 14 px, tracked 1 to 2.5 px, uppercase where the artefact prints it so): ticket and label field captions, table column heads, km markers, Snellen fractions, the postal footer at 12 to 13 px.

### The ten added Google fonts
Each is registered in both `frontend-email-builder/src/lib/fonts.ts` (`FONT_OPTIONS`, `GOOGLE_FONT_SLUG`) and `_GOOGLE_FONT_SLUGS` in `backend/templates_api/export_engine.py`, and is always written with its fallback stack:
- **Archivo** (400 to 800): `'Archivo', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`. Signal/Noise, Paper Plane.
- **Alfa Slab One** (400): `'Alfa Slab One', Rockwell, Georgia, serif`. Fieldbook Seeds.
- **Barlow Condensed** (400 to 700): `'Barlow Condensed', 'Arial Narrow', Arial, sans-serif`. Low Ohm Records.
- **Big Shoulders Stencil** (700, 800): `'Big Shoulders Stencil', 'Arial Narrow', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`. Forge & Temper.
- **Bungee** (400): `'Bungee', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`. Devil's Garage.
- **Courier Prime** (400, 700): `'Courier Prime', 'Courier New', Courier, monospace`. Ledgerline, Paper Plane.
- **EB Garamond** (400, 600, italic 400): `'EB Garamond', Garamond, Georgia, serif`. Harrow & Vale.
- **Overpass** (400 to 800): `'Overpass', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`. Summit Supply.
- **Press Start 2P** (400): `'Press Start 2P', Monaco, Consolas, 'Lucida Console', 'Courier New', monospace`. Night Shift Arcade.
- **Silkscreen** (400, 700): `'Silkscreen', Monaco, Consolas, 'Lucida Console', 'Courier New', monospace`. Madder & Indigo.

### Named Rules
**The Heading-Block Type Rule.** Distinctive type is set only on heading blocks (`style.fontFamily`, `fontSize`, `fontWeight`, `letterSpacing`, `textTransform`), because the rich-text editor strips inline styles. Text blocks carry body copy in the template's default font with only per-block `fontSize` and `color`.

**The Fallback Is the Design Rule.** Web fonts load only in Apple Mail, iOS Mail and some Android clients. Every family is written with a fallback stack chosen to keep the world (stencil to Arial Narrow, pixel faces to Monaco/Consolas, slab to Rockwell, Garamond to Garamond/Georgia), and the `.nofonts` render must still read as the brand.

## Layout

A single centred column on an outer ground: `contentWidth` 600 px, with 560 px for the Paper Plane label on kraft and 380 px for the Margin Books receipt slip. Templates are body-only; header and footer regions stay empty because the editor canvas never renders them, so the masthead and the postal footer are ordinary body blocks.

The content inset is 40 px left and right on every block (`spacing.inset`); 32 px at 560 width (`inset-560`) and 24 px on the 380 px receipt (`inset-receipt`). Where the artefact draws its own frame, the inset sits inside it: Devil's Garage panels sit in 14/572/14 column rows (`panel-frame`), and Night Shift's screen content uses 32 to 48 px. Vertical rhythm is set per block padding, typically 24 to 48 px between sections, with spacer blocks for fixed gaps and coloured spacer bands (10 px route or packet band, 8 px blaze stripes, 6 px heat bar) as structural rules.

Columns carry no gap by default (`column-gap` 0 in 99 of 111 rows); the ratio does the spacing. **Data rows** (prices, times, scores, ticket fields, ledger entries, table rows) use `stackOnMobile: false` so they stay aligned on a 390 px phone. **Inset rows** (image beside text, paired buttons, product triplets) stack, and put the inset as outer-column padding (40 px left on the first column, 40 px right on the last); stacked, the engine gives every column the row's widest side padding on both sides, so the inset holds after collapse.

### Named Rules
**The Data Rows Don't Stack Rule.** Any row whose columns are read across (time / session, item / price, rank / initials / score, label / value) sets `stackOnMobile: false`.

**The Outer-Padding Inset Rule.** A stacking row takes its inset from outer-column padding, never from a block-level wrapper, so the engine can mirror it symmetrically when stacked.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. No template uses a shadow, and the engine targets clients (Outlook desktop's Word engine, Gmail) where shadows do not render. Depth comes from the outer ground against the page, from full-bleed colour slabs set as heading or column backgrounds (the ticket header, the programme headline slab, the packet masthead), and from ruled structure: double, dashed and solid divider blocks and coloured spacer bands.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Ground Rule.** Separation is made with ground colour, full-width bands and rules from the artefact (ledger double rules, receipt tear lines, stitch dashes), never with shadow or blur.

## Shapes

Square. `bodyBorderRadius` is 0 in every template and every button, column and image has radius 0 (`rounded.none`), matching Outlook desktop, which ignores radius. Round forms appear only where the artefact is round, and then as authored rasters (the Northline stop markers, the VU meter faces). Rules are the main geometry: solid, dashed (receipt, spec sheet, stitch, tear line) and double (ledger), plus narrow column cells used as solid stripes (the 20 px Northline spine, the 14 px provenance ribbon).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** square, radius 0 in every template.
- **Primary:** the brand's artefact colour with its page or ink as text, 12 to 18 px vertical by 22 to 34 px horizontal padding (`components.button-<brand>`), set in the brand's display face where the artefact would print it so (Bungee, Big Shoulders Stencil, Press Start 2P, Alfa Slab One, Overpass, Barlow Condensed) and in the body face otherwise. Labels are verbs in the brand's voice (Open your books, Reserve one of 36, Fill the tank, Sign up to play).
- **Secondary:** an outline variant only where a second action is real: Alpenbahn's Manage booking (2 px #111111 on white) and Harrow & Vale's Register to bid (2 px ribbon red on buff), both paired full-width with the primary in a stacking 50/50 row.
- **Hover / Focus:** none authored; email clients do not apply them.

### Data Rows
- **Structure:** a non-stacking columns block per row, divider blocks between rows, cells as heading blocks (so a label, a value and a numeral can each carry type) or text blocks (receipt lines, spec values).
- **Variants:** ledger entries (18/58/24), programme sessions (22/78), sowing table (50/28/22 with zebra column backgrounds), ticket fields (36/32/32), receipt lines (70/30), high scores (30/25/45), tour dates (30/44/26), spec sheet (30/70).

### Artefact Strips
- **Structure:** a three-column row whose narrow middle column is a solid colour cell holding an authored raster: Northline's 90/20/490 stop rows on a Line Yellow spine, Harrow & Vale's 100/14/486 provenance rows with a ribbon-red or gap-buff cell. Decorative strip rasters carry empty alt text.

### Postal Footer
- **Style:** a text block at 12 to 13 px in the brand's secondary ink: legal name and real-format postal address; marketing and welcome emails add why the reader is receiving it and an unsubscribe or preferences text link, transactional ones (ticket, receipt, label) carry terms or returns instead.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build each template from one artefact of the brand's world and commit its colours at page scale.
- **Do** keep templates body-only: header and footer regions empty, masthead and postal footer as body blocks.
- **Do** set distinctive type on heading blocks, always with its email fallback stack; leave text blocks to the default font with only `fontSize` and `color`.
- **Do** inset content 40 px (32 px at 560 width, 24 px on the 380 px receipt), and give stacking rows that inset as outer-column padding.
- **Do** set `stackOnMobile: false` on every data row.
- **Do** use owned rasters under `frontend/public/gallery-assets/<brand>/` with provenance embedded and meaningful alt text; letters and numerals inside art are authored rasters (the sampler alphabet, the route-map labels, the barcode), never AI-rendered text.
- **Do** write realistic sample values: names, addresses, prices, codes, tracking numbers, dates.
- **Do** keep body text and secondary ink at 4.5:1 or better against their surface.
- **Do** check every template in its `.nofonts` render; the system-font view must still read in-world.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use social or icon blocks: their SVG data URIs do not display in Gmail or Outlook. Use text links.
- **Don't** use `html` blocks in gallery templates.
- **Don't** use merge tags (`{{first_name}}`) in gallery templates.
- **Don't** put inline styles or font families inside text-block rich text; the editor strips them.
- **Don't** round corners or add shadows; Outlook desktop drops both and no artefact here needs them.
- **Don't** put an eyebrow label above a heading. Field captions that belong to the artefact (ticket and label fields, table column heads) sit with their value, not as kickers over a headline.
- **Don't** open with a full-bleed photo hero with centred copy, a row of icon cards, a dark CTA slab or an italic quote.
- **Don't** share a palette, type voice or layout formula between two templates.
- **Don't** hotlink or use expiring image URLs.
